About Us

Mambo Grill & Café is a locally owned restaurant serving freshly prepared meals to the Ruai community and visitors passing through the area.

What Makes Us Special

  • Fresh Food

    Freshly prepared meals made with quality ingredients.

  • Affordable Prices

    Delicious food at prices that fit your budget.

  • Friendly Service

    Warm, welcoming service that makes every visit enjoyable.

  • Local Atmosphere

    A relaxed, vibrant setting inspired by the warmth of Kenyan hospitality.

A Warm Space for Every Occasion

Step into a welcoming atmosphere designed for good food, great company, and memorable moments. Whether you're enjoying a family meal, catching up with friends, or simply grabbing a bite, Mambo Grill & Café offers a comfortable space where you can relax and feel right at home.

Customer's Favorite: Nyama Choma

Tender, smoky, and packed with authentic flavor. Served hot with kachumbari and your choice of ugali or fries, it's a taste that keeps our customers coming back.
